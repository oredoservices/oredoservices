How to use Oredo Services

  1. Sign up with your phone

    Verify your number with a one-time SMS code and complete your profile in under a minute.

  2. Add your place

    Pin your address in Shebin El Qanater on the map, so pros know exactly where to go.

  3. Create your order

    Say what you need and from which shop, add your notes, and set the price you want to pay.

  4. Pick the offer you like

    Pros nearby accept your price or propose theirs. Compare names, ratings and prices, then accept one.

  5. Follow it live

    Chat with your pro or call them, and watch your order move from accepted to in progress to done.

  6. Pay and rate

    Pay the agreed price in cash, plus the cost of any items bought for you, then rate your pro.

Plumbing, electrical work and cleaning

  1. Describe the job

    Pick the type (for example maintenance, installation or finishing, or the place to clean), write what you need and add photos if you like.

  2. Choose your pro

    Pros in your area apply. Open each profile, check the rating and previous work, and pick one.

  3. Agree on the price in person

    The pro comes for the inspection, and you agree on the price face to face.

Oredo Pro The app for service providers

Your skill. Your hours. Your price.

Oredo Pro brings you delivery, plumbing, electrical and cleaning requests from clients in the parts of Shebin El Qanater you choose. Work on your own hours and get paid in cash, with no commission during launch.

  • No commission during launch: the agreed price is all yours
  • No shifts: switch to available whenever it suits you
  • Delivery: accept the client's price or propose yours. Other services: apply and agree on the price with the client
  • Payment goes straight to you, in cash
  • Track your completed orders and earnings
  • Build a rating that wins you more orders
  • Plumbers and electricians: show your previous work to win more clients

How to start with Oredo Pro

  1. Sign up with your phone

    Download Oredo Pro, verify your number and complete your profile with a clear photo of yourself.

  2. Choose your service and areas

    Pick delivery, plumbing, electrical work or cleaning, and the parts of Shebin El Qanater you want requests from.

  3. Upload your documents

    Your national ID (front and back) for every service. Delivery: the criminal record certificate and your vehicle type (motorcycle, tuk-tuk or bicycle). Plumbing and electrical work: 2 to 6 photos of your previous work and a short bio if you like. Cleaning: the ID is enough.

  4. Get approved

    Your documents are reviewed, and you get a notification as soon as your account is approved.

  5. Get requests

    New requests near you arrive as notifications. Delivery: accept the client's price or propose yours. Other services: apply, and the client chooses.

  6. Do the job and get paid

    Do the work with care, keep any receipts, and collect your cash from the client.
