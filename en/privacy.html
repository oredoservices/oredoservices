1Who this policy covers

  • Clients — people who use the Oredo Services app to request services (such as delivery, plumbing, electrical work and cleaning) from service providers.
  • Pros — independent service providers who use the Oredo Pro app to receive and carry out requests.

One person can have one client account and one pro account. Each account is separate, and its data is handled as described below.

Client data

  • Saved places: the address you type and the map location (latitude and longitude) of each place you save.
  • Service regions you choose and your default region.
  • Orders: requested items, store names, notes, pickup location, initial price, offers received, agreed price, status history and timestamps.
  • On-site job requests (plumbing, electrical work and cleaning): for plumbing and electrical work, the request type (maintenance, installation or finishing) and your description; for cleaning, the place type (apartment, villa or duplex, office or shop, building stairs) and its details (cleaning type, number of rooms, bathrooms or floors, whether the place is furnished, its approximate area, whether the building entrance is included, and who brings the cleaning supplies) and optional notes. For all three: optional photos of the fault or the place (up to 6, depending on the service and request type), the chosen saved address, the pros who applied, status history and timestamps. These requests have no price in the app.
  • Ratings you give to pros, and ratings pros give to you.
  • Messages in order chats with the pro handling your order, and in Support chat with us.

Pro data

  • Service type you provide and the regions you work in.
  • Verification documents: photos of your national ID (front and back) and, only for services that require it, your criminal record certificate.
  • Vehicle information (only for services that need a vehicle): the vehicle type (motorcycle, tuk-tuk or bicycle). No plate number or licence photo is collected.
  • Previous work (only for services that need it, such as plumbing and electrical work): 2 to 6 photos of past jobs and an optional short bio (up to 500 characters). The team reviews them before clients can see them.
  • Availability: whether you are Online / Available. Delivery pros share their location while Online. Plumbers, electricians and cleaners share only an Available / Unavailable status, never their location.
  • Verification status and any review notes.
  • Work activity: orders you receive, offers you send, prices, completed orders, earnings summaries, ratings, and the orders you cancelled after being assigned (so that order is not offered to you again).
  • Messages in order chats with clients, and in Support chat with us.

Data collected automatically

  • Device push token and app version, used to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Crash and diagnostic reports (Firebase Crashlytics): device model, operating system version and technical details of app errors.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): screens used and app events, used in aggregate to improve the app.
  • App integrity signals (Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity), used to confirm requests come from the genuine app and not from bots or modified copies.

4Who can see your data

The other party of an active order

To make an order possible, the client and the pro handling the same order can see each other's:

  • name, profile photo and rating;
  • phone number, so they can call each other about the order;
  • the order details, including the pickup location and address.

Pros can never see clients' saved places that are not part of their order. Clients can never see pros' verification documents.

Pros an open request was sent to

Before a pro is assigned, the pros in the request's region who were sent it see your name and photo, the region, the address and the request details (for on-site jobs: the type, details, description and photos).

Clients choosing a pro for an on-site job

The pro's public profile (name, photo, rating, member-since date, and the approved bio and previous-work photos where the service uses them) is visible to the client when the pro applies to their request.

The team

Only the team behind the apps, or a person the team authorises to help run the apps, can access data, and only when needed: reviewing pro documents, answering support requests, cancelling an order when there is a problem, or investigating a reported problem. Every order cancellation by the team is recorded with its reason.

Service providers that process data for us

We use Google Firebase (Google LLC) to host and run the apps: sign-in, database, file storage, notifications, crash reporting, analytics and app integrity. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own security and privacy commitments, and may store it on servers outside Egypt.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not share your personal data with advertisers, marketers or any other third party for their own purposes.
  • We only disclose data to authorities when Egyptian law requires it, and only the data the request legally covers.

5How we protect your data

  • All data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections (TLS).
  • Data is encrypted at rest on Google Cloud servers.
  • Strict access rules on the database and file storage: each account can only read and change its own private data, and verification documents are not readable by other users.
  • Sign-in requires verifying your phone number with a one-time code.
  • Only one device session is active per account; signing in on a new device ends the old session.
  • App integrity checks block requests that do not come from the genuine app.
  • Sensitive values stored on your phone are kept in encrypted storage.
  • Access by the team, or anyone the team authorises, is limited to what each task needs.

No system is 100% secure. If we become aware of a breach that affects your data, we will notify you and the competent authorities as the law requires.

6How long we keep data, and account deletion

While your account is active

We keep your data while your account is active, because it is needed to provide the service.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete account in the app. Deletion is not possible while you have an open order; finish or cancel it first.

When you delete an account, the following is deleted automatically:

  • your name, phone number, profile photo, bio and previous-work photos, which are removed from your public profile;
  • all photos and files you uploaded (profile photo, ID photos, criminal record certificate, request photos and previous-work photos);
  • your saved places (clients) or your verification record, availability status and pending offers or applications (pros);
  • your notifications and your Support chat;
  • your device tokens, so you stop receiving notifications.

If you have no other active account in either app (client or pro), your sign-in identity is also deleted: phone number, email, gender and date of birth. Your phone number and email become free to register again.

What remains after deletion

Records of past orders, the messages in those order chats, and ratings stay in our system. They are needed for the other party's history, for accounting, and for handling disputes. These records are no longer linked to your name, photo or phone number; you appear as "Deleted user". The text of messages you sent in order chats stays visible to the other party of that order.

Crash reports and analytics are kept by Google Firebase in aggregate form for the retention period set in those services. They do not include your name, phone or documents.

7Your rights

Under Egyptian law you may:

  • access your data — most of it is visible in your account;
  • correct your data — edit your profile in the app, or ask Support;
  • delete your data — delete your account in the app (Section 6);
  • withdraw consent — turn off notifications or location in the app or your phone settings, or delete your account;
  • object to or ask about any processing — contact Support.

We answer requests within a reasonable time and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

8Age requirement

The apps are only for people aged 16 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If we learn that an account belongs to someone under 16, we will delete it.

We may update this policy. We will show the new version in the app, and when the changes are significant we will ask you to accept them again before you continue using the app. The "Version" number at the top shows the latest version.
