1What Oredo Services is

1.1Oredo Services is a technology platform. It connects clients who need a service (such as delivery, plumbing, electrical work or cleaning) with independent pros who provide it.

1.2Oredo Services is not a delivery, transport, maintenance, cleaning or service company, and it is not a party to the agreement between a client and a pro. The pro provides the service on their own responsibility.

1.3Pros are independent. They are not employees, agents or representatives of Oredo Services, and no employment relationship exists between Oredo Services and any pro.

1.4Oredo Services and Oredo Pro are app names, not a company. The apps are owned and operated by Egyptian software engineers, who are the other party to these Terms.

2Eligibility and accounts

2.1You must be at least 16 years old and legally able to enter into a contract.

2.2You must give true, accurate and current information and keep it up to date.

2.3You may have one client account and one pro account. Accounts are personal: do not share them, sell them, or let anyone else use them.

2.4You are responsible for all activity on your account and for keeping your phone and verification codes secure.

3Pro verification and documents

3.1To receive orders, a pro must upload clear, genuine and valid documents: national ID (front and back) and any other document the chosen service requires, such as a criminal record certificate, the vehicle type for services that need a vehicle, or photos of previous work. Previous work must be the pro's own.

3.2The pro is solely responsible for the authenticity, validity and legality of every document and licence they upload, and for renewing any document that expires.

3.3Oredo Services reviews documents visually but does not guarantee their authenticity. Oredo Services is not responsible for any document or licence that is forged, altered, expired, invalid or belongs to someone else, or for any consequence of using one.

3.4Uploading forged or false documents leads to immediate and permanent account suspension. Oredo Services may report the matter to the competent authorities.

4Pro obligations, safety and care of orders

4.1Your safety comes first. No order is worth risking your life. You must:

  • obey all traffic laws and speed limits;
  • wear a helmet and the required safety gear when riding a motorcycle or bicycle;
  • never use your phone while driving — only use the app when you have safely stopped;
  • not drive while tired, ill, or under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any substance that affects driving;
  • avoid unsafe roads and weather conditions when possible, and never rush in a way that puts you or others in danger;
  • keep your vehicle roadworthy and hold every licence and insurance the law requires.

4.2Care of orders. For delivery, you must:

  • handle every order with care and deliver it complete and undamaged;
  • not open, tamper with, swap or use any item of an order;
  • keep the purchase receipt and the remaining money (if any) and hand them to the client;
  • tell the client immediately about any problem, delay or unavailable item.

For plumbing, electrical work and cleaning, you must:

  • arrive at the agreed address;
  • inspect the job before agreeing on a price;
  • do the work safely and to a professional standard;
  • respect the client's home or premises.

4.3Conduct. Be respectful and honest with clients. Charge only the price agreed with the client, and do not ask for extra payments.

4.4You must refuse any order for illegal, dangerous or prohibited items (Section 6).

4.5You are solely responsible for any accident, injury, damage, fine or loss that happens while you are providing a service.

5Client obligations

5.1Give an accurate address, location and order description, including accurate photos and details for on-site jobs, and be reachable on your phone while the order is active.

5.2Only request lawful items and services.

5.3Pay the pro the agreed price: for delivery, plus the cost of the purchased items on delivery; for other services, when the job is done.

5.4Treat pros with respect. Abuse, harassment or threats lead to account suspension.

6Prohibited use

Users must not use the apps to:

  • request or deliver illegal items, weapons, drugs, alcohol where prohibited, hazardous materials, or anything whose transport is restricted by law;
  • harass, threaten, insult, defraud or harm any person;
  • give false information, impersonate anyone, or create fake accounts or fake orders;
  • manipulate ratings, prices or offers;
  • copy, reverse-engineer, attack or overload the apps or their servers;
  • collect other users' data, or use it for anything other than the order it was shared for.

7Prices, payment and cancellation

7.1For delivery, the price is agreed through the offers in the app. For plumbing, electrical work and cleaning, there is no price in the app: the client and the pro agree on it in person after the inspection.

7.2Payment is made directly between the client and the pro (for example, in cash on delivery). The apps do not process payments.

7.3Oredo Services is not responsible for payment disputes between users. Support may try to help resolve a dispute, but has no obligation to.

7.4Cancellation. A pro may cancel an assigned order before it is completed. The order then goes back to other pros, and it is not offered again to the pro who cancelled. A client may cancel an on-site job until the pro is on the way. The team may cancel an order when there is a problem, and records the reason.
